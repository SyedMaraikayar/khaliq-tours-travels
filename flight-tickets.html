Flight ticket assistance

Flight tickets, confirmed when you enquire.

Domestic and international routes from Coimbatore and other cities. We check options with you. There is no live search on this page.

What to send us

A useful flight enquiry includes the points below. You can edit the WhatsApp message before it is sent.

  • Departure city or airport
  • Destination city or airport
  • Travel date, and return date if you need one
  • One-way or return
  • Number of passengers
  • Cabin preference, if you have one
Enquire on WhatsApp Call Us Email

Airline fares and seats change. The fare we discuss is the one available at the time of your enquiry, not a standing offer.

Flight enquiry questions

Can I search flights and book instantly here?

No. This website is for enquiries. We confirm availability and the applicable fare with you before any booking is finalised.

Will the fare stay the same after I message you?

Not always. Fares can move while we are checking. We tell you the fare that applies before you confirm.

How is a booking confirmed?

After you choose an option, the arrangement is confirmed directly with Khaliq Tours & Travels. Payment and ticket details are handled at that stage, not through this website.

What if I need a change or cancellation?

Rules depend on the airline and the fare. We explain the conditions of the option you are considering. We do not publish a single cancellation policy for every ticket.
