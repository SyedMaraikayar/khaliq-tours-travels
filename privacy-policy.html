Privacy policy

This note explains what happens when you use the Khaliq Tours & Travels website or contact the business. It is not a claim that every privacy law is covered. Extra consent tools may be needed once analytics or advertising tags are switched on.

What you send us

If you use WhatsApp, the phone link, or email, we receive whatever you choose to write: usually a destination, dates, number of travellers, and your contact details. Please do not send passport numbers, card numbers, or copies of identity documents through the website. There is no enquiry form on this site.

How that information is used

We use it to reply to the travel enquiry and to arrange the service you asked about. We do not sell enquiry details.

Analytics and ads

Google Analytics 4 and Google Ads tags are not loaded until measurement IDs are added in the site configuration. When they are added, those services may collect usage data under their own policies. A WhatsApp click or a phone click is recorded as an enquiry action, not as a completed booking.
