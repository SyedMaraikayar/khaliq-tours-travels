Visa and tours

Visa guidance and trips shaped around you.

Two separate services. Neither is a fixed package list, and neither promises a visa outcome.

International visa assistance

We help you understand the documents and steps commonly required for a destination. Requirements, fees, processing times and decisions vary by country and by the authority handling the application.

We are not an embassy and we do not guarantee approval or a processing time.

Tour packages

Tour enquiries are customised. Tell us the destination, rough dates, who is travelling, and the kind of trip you want. We discuss flights, stay and local arrangements only where they are available for that request.

There is no catalogue of fixed departures, hotel names, inclusions or discounts on this website. A price is discussed for your enquiry, not published in advance.

Questions

Do you guarantee visa approval?

No. The decision belongs to the relevant authority. We can help you prepare an enquiry and understand published requirements.

Where is the package price list?

There isn’t one. Tours are quoted after we know the destination, dates and group. Availability has to be checked first.

Can one enquiry cover both a visa and the trip?

Yes. Say so in the message and we will treat them as related parts of the same request.
